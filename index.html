About Us

More Than a Last Name

To me, Grimes has always meant more than just a last name.

When Wayne “Papa” Grimes became part of my nana’s life, he also chose to become a father to my mom, April “Mama” Grimes, and my aunt, Tami “Gogo” Grimes. He adopted them both without hesitation, loved them as his own, and gave them the Grimes name — a name I’m incredibly proud to carry today.

That story has shaped what family means to me. Family isn’t always defined by blood. Sometimes it’s the people who choose you, show up for you, love you, and become family along the way.

That same meaning carries into The Grimes & Company. We are proudly family owned and operated, but not everyone who makes up our family is related by blood. Some are relatives, some are friends, and some are people who simply became family through the years.

This company carries the Grimes name because to me, that name represents love, loyalty, hard work, and choosing to show up for the people around you.

Papa Wayne gave our family the Grimes name, and it’s a name I’m incredibly proud to carry, build on, and one day pass down to my own children.
